# Asset Briefs - 2026-05-09

Scope: T125-T129 production briefs and curation rules for current and future Liarline visual assets.

## Case Lab Hero

- Use: briefing pressure image behind first suspect/case hook.
- Brief: dark university robotics lab, forensic amber highlights, no police cliche.
- Reject: readable fake text, watermarks, new clue props, bright stock-photo lighting, low contrast.

## Interrogation Background

- Use: dark background under interrogation overlay.
- Brief: subdued lab monitors and terminal pressure, no UI screenshots or bokeh.
- Reject: noisy texture, bright gradients, focal points behind transcript, horizontal crop problems.

## Evidence Paper

- Use: Notebook clue slips.
- Brief: warm forensic paper with subtle grain and high text contrast.
- Reject: legible fake handwriting, official seals, watermarks, high-noise stains.

## Suspect Portraits

- Use: suspect grid, active suspect card, persona shift.
- Brief: centered face, readable eyes at 56-80px, dark low-detail background, same lighting family.
- Reject: plastic skin, distorted eyes/hands, badges/logos, overt guilt/innocence cues, style drift.

## Icon Treatment

- Use: buttons, evidence tags, warning/risk states.
- Brief: Lucide React only, 2px stroke, 20-22px controls, 44px hit target.
- Reject: mixed icon families, red icons for non-danger states, icon-only primary actions.

## Future AI Illustration Rules

- Generate one role at a time from its own brief.
- Curate before use: watermark, fake text, anatomy, crop, contrast, spoiler, style consistency.
- Store raw/rejected outputs outside the public bundle.
- Add new images only if they clarify deduction or state; do not turn gameplay into a gallery.

## Required Asset Checks

- no visible watermark
- no readable fake text
- mobile crop safe
- style consistent
- no story spoiler
- within public PNG size budget
