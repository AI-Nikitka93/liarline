# Phase 6 Scenario And Mood Asset Briefs

Date: 2026-05-09

## Production Rule

Additional visuals are allowed only when they clarify a deduction state. They must stay as small gameplay panels, not gallery images, hero replacements, or decorative AI art.

## Scenario Inserts

1. Briefing tension
   - Use: small lab panel behind the first suspect and time-window pressure.
   - Reject: extra clue props, readable fake text, police/court imagery, generic neon dashboard.

2. First AI hesitation
   - Use: restrained interrogation texture while the answer is pending.
   - Reject: fake live-success language, spinner-only loading, decorative waveform without state meaning.

3. Contradiction reveal
   - Use: evidence-paper/case-board insert showing that camera timing and cart movement now conflict.
   - Reject: culprit reveal, motive reveal, new evidence not in the deterministic case.

4. Persona shift
   - Use: Ivo-focused pressure portrait panel after contradiction.
   - Reject: villain face, guilt proof, horror panic, exaggerated AI artifacts.

5. Accusation risk
   - Use: signed proof-chain surface beside final risk and selected evidence.
   - Reject: hiding disabled states, making final submit look like a normal chat send.

6. Resolution
   - Use: closing lab/reconstruction panel.
   - Reject: promising more cases, multiplayer, trial mode, or future scope as current gameplay.

## Mood Visual System

- controlled: stable neutral line, no motion.
- nervous: amber timing rail, restrained hesitation.
- defensive: red border pressure, no guilt implication.
- impatient: cyan clipped state, fast witness tone without danger semantics.
- shaken: amber/red split after contradiction.
- panicking: red pressure pulse only after persona shift.

## Asset Acceptance

- Existing PNG release assets remain the only current production assets.
- Future asset generation must be one asset per role, with manual selection and mobile crop proof.
- Any insert must pass no watermark, no readable fake text, no spoiler prop, no style drift, and no broken 360px crop.
